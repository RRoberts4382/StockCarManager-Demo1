# Riverside Oval references

Riverside Oval is the fictional half-mile asphalt layout stored as track ID 1. Its original model uses 20-degree turns, 5-degree straights, and a nominal 9 m width.

## Estimated qualifying reference

Use New Smyrna Speedway as a comparison because it is also a banked half-mile asphalt oval. NASCAR lists 23-degree turns there, and the published track is wider than the fictional Riverside layout. The comparison gives Riverside a reference pace of 17.734 seconds at 101.499944 mph. The time comes from Tristan McKee's second qualifying lap in the 2026 ASA STARS National Tour event. Riverside's reference is a game estimate.
